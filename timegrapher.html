Nominal guide

General workshop guidance for mechanical watches — not a substitute for the movement maker’s specification.

Rate±15 s/dayGenerally a good practical target for a serviced vintage mechanical watch. Context matters.
Beat error≤ 0.6 msA useful general target. Around 1.0 ms or more usually deserves closer attention.
Confidence80%+Strong reading. Below ~60%, improve microphone contact or reduce surrounding noise before trusting it.
Positions6 possibleDU, DD, crown up/down/left/right. Use at least two positions for a quick check.
Amplitude is intentionally not shown. Calibre will not invent an amplitude figure from microphone loudness; it will only be added if the signal model can measure it defensibly.
