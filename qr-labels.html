Printer not Android-compatible?

Use Share / export PNG. Calibre creates the label image itself, then sends it to Android’s share sheet. From there you can open it in the printer manufacturer’s app, a compatible print app, or save it for printing from another device.

System print still works for Android/DeX printers exposed through the browser. The PNG route avoids requiring the browser to talk directly to the printer.
