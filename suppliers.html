Where to buy

Measure first. Height, strength and length for a spring. Pivot size for a staff. Do not order on jewel count.

Australia first

Australian Watch & Clock Supplies, Sydney. Pocket mainsprings, crystals, bows, stems. A watchmaker will talk through the size.
australianwatchclocksupplies.com.au

Australian Jewellers Supplies. Pocket springs are listed, but branch stock moves. Check before you rely on it.
jewellerssupplies.com.au

All Time Co. ETA, Sellita, Seiko and ordinary case parts. Weak for an Olma staff.
alltimeco.com.au

Overseas, when nobody here has it

Cousins UK. Swiss pocket material, white-alloy springs by size, Bergeon. Ships to Hobart.
cousinsuk.com

Otto Frei. Old American and Swiss material lists. Small parts orders can go by international mail.
ofrei.com

Jules Borel. Tools and modern material. Same Oakland house as Otto Frei.
julesborel.com

Not a catalogue

A matching hand, a bow of the right wire, or an obsolete staff usually comes from a donor movement, not a listing. Buy the movement. Crown Vintage in Sydney is dials and bracelets, not a parts bench.

Write the supplier and the size on the stage that used the part. The cost goes on Business.
